PECAN

Programming Encoder Classification Analysis Network: encoder models that identify a code snippet's programming language across hundreds of languages.

Overview

PECAN asks whether fine-tuned encoder models can identify a snippet's programming language accurately across hundreds of languages, and how they compare with established tools and a large language model.

What I did

  • Trained encoder models on 28M+ samples spanning 319 languages, reaching 95.8% accuracy.
  • Benchmarked them against GPT-OSS-120B, GitHub Linguist, highlight.js, and Guesslang.
  • Retrained a Guesslang baseline on our data to separate gains from the architecture from gains from the data.
  • Built scalable training and evaluation pipelines with distributed training and hyperparameter tuning.
  • Ran error analysis to find model weaknesses and improve robustness.

Status

I defended my honors thesis on PECAN in May 2026 and presented it at the Stanford Research Conference in April 2026. A paper is in preparation.
