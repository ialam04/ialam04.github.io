Research & Selected Work

Clinician-in-the-loop active learning for Parkinson's freezing of gait

VisuaLab, UNC Chapel Hill · Advisor: Dr. Danielle Szafir · Aug 2026 – present

Designing an active learning system that helps clinicians label precursor signs of freezing of gait, extending a CHI 2024 visual analytics platform for clinical motion data. I'm studying how interface order, showing the model's prediction first versus the raw data first, shapes expert labeling, downstream model calibration, and overreliance on wrong predictions.

Active learning Human-AI interaction Calibration

PECAN: Programming Encoder Classification Analysis Network

AISX Lab, LSU · Advisor: Dr. James Ghawaly · Sep 2024 – present

Encoder models that identify a code snippet's programming language. I trained them on 28M+ samples across 319 languages and benchmarked them against GPT-OSS-120B, Linguist, highlight.js, and Guesslang, including a Guesslang baseline retrained on our data to separate architecture gains from data gains. A paper is in preparation.

95.8% accuracy 319 languages 28M+ training samples
